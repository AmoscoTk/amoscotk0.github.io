Credit Risk PD Model — Deployment-Grade

Volatility

Crypto Volatility & Risk Forecaster

Next-day BTC realised volatility forecasting using HAR, LightGBM, and EWMA, with rigorous VaR backtesting.

  • Volatility targeting overlay: exposure scaled inversely to forecast vol

Reinforcement Learning

RL Trading — Multi-Asset Continuous

PPO, SAC, and TD3 agents trained on a custom multi-asset Gymnasium environment with continuous target-weight actions and turnover costs in the reward.

  • Causal timing: weights at t earn t to t+1 returns; no look-ahead
  • Seed-averaged evaluation across four PPO runs to report Sharpe distribution
  • Benchmarked against buy-and-hold, equal-weight, and momentum baseline
  • Honest conclusion: RL value comes from risk-aware sizing, not directional alpha
